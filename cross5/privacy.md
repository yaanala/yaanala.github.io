# Cross 5 Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 1 November 2026
**Developer:** Yaanala
**Contact:** yesmynameisyogesh@gmail.com

## In short

Cross 5 has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. We do not run a server that receives your data, and the app does not send anything about you to us. Everything the app remembers stays on your phone.

## What the app stores on your phone

- Your settings: theme, board style, sound, vibration and default game options.
- Player names you type for Pass & Play, and the name you choose to show a friend in nearby games.
- Your results against the computer: rating, streaks, stars and achievements.

This information stays on your device. Cross 5 does not back it up. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes it.

## Playing with a friend nearby

**On the same Wi-Fi or a hotspot.** The two phones talk to each other directly. What is sent: the moves, the name you chose, and a game code that contains part of your phone's local network address. This travels only between the two phones and never reaches us. Games on Wi-Fi are not encrypted, so play on a network you trust.

**Over Bluetooth.** Cross 5 uses Google's Nearby Connections service, which is part of Google Play services. While you wait for a friend, your phone's model name can be seen by other Cross 5 phones close by, and both players confirm a short code before the connection starts. Google handles this connection under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Bluetooth play is only offered on phones that have Google Play services.

## Camera

The camera is used only when you tap "Scan their QR code" to join a friend's game. It reads the code and nothing else: the picture is not saved and is not sent anywhere by Cross 5. On phones with Google Play services the scan is done by Google's code scanner; on other phones Cross 5 reads the code itself. You can always type the code instead.

## Permissions and why we ask

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Camera | To scan a friend's QR code. Optional. |
| Nearby devices / Bluetooth | To find and connect to a friend's phone over Bluetooth. |
| Location (Android 12 and older only) | Android requires it to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices. Cross 5 never reads your location. |
| Wi-Fi and network state | To find your Wi-Fi address and connect to a friend on the same network. |
| Local network (Android 17 and newer) | To reach a friend's phone on the same Wi-Fi. |
| Internet | To connect to a friend's phone over your local network. Cross 5 does not contact any server of ours. |

## Children

Cross 5 does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children. Players can type any name they like. That name is shown only to the friend they play with.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever adds accounts, ads, analytics or online play, this policy will be updated before that release.
